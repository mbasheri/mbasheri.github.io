Plateau

In progress

Fitness apps chart your progress and stop there — when a lift stalls, you get a flat line and no explanation. Plateau reads the training log alongside sleep, stress, and bodyweight, then works out the likely reason the lift stopped moving: not enough stimulus, accumulated fatigue, stale programming, or under-eating. It returns a written diagnosis and a specific change to make, not just a flag.
